About Me

I fell in love with video games as a kid and, honestly, never really grew out of it. GTA: Vice City eventually made me curious about what was happening behind the screen—rendering, shaders, engine systems, and all the invisible things that somehow make a world feel real.

I studied Industrial Engineering, but my path gradually drifted toward computer science and game development through teaching, studio work, and probably many side projects. Since then, I’ve worked with Unreal Engine, Unity, C++, and OpenGL, exploring both the technical and artistic sides of games. That mix is what I love most: building something complex under the hood, then turning it into something players can actually see, feel, and hopefully remember.
